<h2>🧑‍💻 About Me</h2>

I started with **mathematics**, moved into computer science, and found my passion in **Artificial Intelligence**.

With two Master's degrees in **Data Science & Intelligent Systems** and **Signal & Image Processing**, I enjoy exploring how intelligent algorithms can solve real-world problems — from understanding complex 3D biological images to building applications that can read, retrieve, and reason over documents.

My journey has taken me through research laboratories, data science projects, and AI engineering challenges. What motivates me most is not just training models, but turning them into useful and reliable solutions.

- 🤖 Exploring **Generative AI, LLMs, RAG architectures & AI-powered applications**

- 🧠 Applying Machine Learning and Deep Learning to real-world challenges
